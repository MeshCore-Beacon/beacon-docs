## Measured limits and remaining gates

Cold global Topology still needs optimization qualification. One public cold
24-hour request took 13.877 seconds, then 0.336 seconds cached. A later local
Pi check crossed a minute boundary and measured 5.8027 and 5.2405 seconds for
two different cold snapshots, followed by a same-window cache hit in 0.0316
seconds. The new historical-decryption job was progressing; CMI and packet-sites
were complete. These are individual shared-host samples, not p95 or FPS claims.

Path deduplication before edge expansion returned identical edge sets on the
restored dataset, but showed no consistent warm speedup. That query change was
not shipped. Measure query-family cost and cache occupancy before choosing a
bounded shared snapshot/precomputation model. Preserve explicit as-of/window
metadata and exact expiry semantics; avoid per-browser pagination fanout.

Private metrics/statement/IO instrumentation and scrapes, whole-host pool/memory
budgets, least-privilege migration/runtime roles, broker-to-database replay and
durability, production RPO/RTO and a full maintenance-cycle soak remain explicit
operator/design work. Sender-time versus receipt-time policy and bounded Atlas
summary/downsampling contracts also remain follow-ups. The current patch does
not make these guarantees by adding counters or passing fixture tests.

No Android debugging device was available for this revision. Fresh physical
BLE/Wi-Fi, screen-off/unlock/reconnect, notification controls, mobile RF-to-intake
upload and overnight qualification remain pending. Keep the app draft. A
cross-client RF coordination policy is also needed before broad mobile/headless
coexistence; signed enrollment is not hardware attestation or sensor truth.

Promote core before optional collection. Rehearse against the actual target
schema/config/roles, deploy a paired immutable candidate to authorized dev/dev1,
and qualify the remaining workload/recovery gates before operators approve a
stable live release. Retained preview recovery copies do not authorize a
database restore over new traffic or a production deployment.
